About / the short version

USIVA is a planning companion, not a decision-maker.

The U.S. Immigration and Visa Association gives individuals, families, employers, and organizations a clearer place to organize records, timing, authorization questions, and source checks across the visa categories that may fit their purpose.

See planning lanesBrowse visa categories

A practical role

Make the question easier to verify.

International planning often holds several timelines at once: academic preparation, funding, travel, school instructions, research opportunities, and the authorization boundaries that apply to each stage. USIVA gives those questions a visible place before deadline pressure makes them harder to separate.

The site is intentionally source-aware. It can help a reader name a missing document, prepare a question for a designated school official or sponsor, and recognize when personal facts require licensed immigration counsel. It does not decide eligibility or replace the authority responsible for a case.

Where USIVA sits

USIVA is a US Council companion. It can sit beside a university, employer, sponsor, or a connected research-program conversation without becoming that provider or promising its outcome.

The editorial standard

Four habits keep a planning page honest.

These are working principles for public information, not conclusions about a particular person’s case.

  1. 01

    Start with the decision

    Ask what must be decided, by whom, and by when before collecting an indiscriminate stack of documents.

  2. 02

    Make the boundary explicit

    Study, training, research exposure, assistantship, travel, and employment can have different rules. Name the activity before assuming it is allowed.

  3. 03

    Leave a source trail

    Public rules, school instructions, sponsor guidance, and professional advice should be checked at the point of use and dated.

  4. 04

    Know when to hand off

    Personal history, a prior refusal, a status concern, or a complicated work question deserves individualized advice from the right authority.

Plain language

What USIVA is — and what it is not.

USIVA is

  • A public planning companion for study, research, family, and employer questions.
  • A place to organize records, timing, source checks, and handoffs.
  • A bridge to official tools and the authority that controls the next answer.

USIVA is not

  • A law firm, legal representative, or eligibility engine.
  • A substitute for university, sponsor, consular, government, or employer instructions.
  • A guarantee of admission, employment, funding, visa issuance, or government approval.
