A trust space, not a promise machine

Make the next step verifiable.

USIVA is informational planning content. It does not file an application, decide eligibility, replace a lawyer or DSO, or guarantee admission, employment, funding, visa issuance, or government approval.

  1. 01

    Ask

    Name the decision in front of you and the facts that could change it.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Use the agency, school, employer, sponsor, or consular source responsible for the rule.

  3. 03

    Hand off

    Take a dated, organized question to the DSO, employer, agency, or qualified counsel who can act.

What a careful file can hold

Records carry the story. Roles carry the responsibility.

Visa questions are rarely only about a form. They are about people, work, study, family history, timing, and the organizations that must coordinate a lawful next move.
