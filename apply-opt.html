Internal Use Only 19. Internal Routing After Submission

Application Received

Thank you for submitting your F-1 OPT application. Here is what happens next:

  1. Watch for a confirmation email from info@usiva.org — typically within 24–48 hours.
  2. A USIVA case manager will contact you to review your documents and answer questions about your OPT timeline.
  3. Schedule a DSO check-in to verify your I-20, SEVIS status, and OPT or STEM OPT eligibility.
  4. If applying for STEM OPT, begin I-983 Training Plan preparation and coordinate with your prospective employer for signing and E-Verify confirmation.
  5. Gather your I-20, Employment Authorization Document (EAD) if available, passport, I-94, and any academic documents for the secure case folder.
