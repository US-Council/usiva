How to use a route

A category label starts the conversation; it does not finish it.

  1. 01

    Name the purpose. Separate study, work, transit, family, official travel, and permanent immigration.

  2. 02

    Open the source. Use the linked State Department or USCIS page, then check the responsible post or program.

  3. 03

    Prepare the handoff. Keep dates, documents, owners, and unresolved questions together.

Planning boundary: USIVA catalog pages are informational. They do not determine eligibility, status, work authorization, admissibility, visa issuance, or timing, and they do not replace individualized advice.
