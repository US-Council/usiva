USIVA / E-Verify

E-Verify is a participation workflow, not a shortcut around Form I-9.

For a company, the useful starting point is sequence: understand the current Form I-9 process, confirm whether E-Verify participation applies, then follow the official enrollment and case rules without turning a preliminary result into an immigration conclusion.

Keep the records distinct

Form I-9 and E-Verify meet in a workflow, not in the same legal question.

Form I-9 and E-Verify are related, but they are not interchangeable. The official agencies publish the operating rules; this table is a plain-language orientation only.

The comparison can be scrolled horizontally on a narrow screen.

High-level orientation: Form I-9 and E-Verify
QuestionForm I-9E-Verify
What is it?The employment eligibility verification record completed through the current USCIS form and instructions.An electronic program that participating employers use to compare Form I-9 information with records available to DHS and SSA.
When does it enter?During the covered-hire onboarding process, using the current instructions and acceptable-document lists.After the Form I-9 step, within the timing and participation rules that apply to the enrolled employer.
What does it answer?Whether the required identity and employment-authorization documentation process has been completed.Whether the electronic case receives a current E-Verify result under the program’s records and rules.
What if information does not match?Follow the current Form I-9 instructions and correct errors through the permitted process.Follow the official notice and referral process. An initial mismatch is not the same as a final case result.
Important distinction: E-Verify is not a visa-category finder, immigration-eligibility opinion, or replacement for the Form I-9 process. It does not turn USIVA into an employer’s counsel or compliance officer.

A company-level sequence

Treat each result as a step with an owner.

Use the official E-Verify pages and terms for the details. This sequence is a planning map, not an instruction to create a case on this site.

  1. 01

    Check the current Form I-9 materials.

    Use USCIS I-9 Central and the current instructions before training a hiring team or configuring software.

  2. 02

    Confirm the participation posture.

    Review whether the company is already enrolled, whether a federal contract or another rule affects the analysis, and which access method fits the operation.

  3. 03

    Use the official enrollment or access path.

    Start from E-Verify’s employer hub and enrollment process. Do not submit company or worker information to USIVA.

  4. 04

    Create and manage cases under the program rules.

    Follow the official verification workflow, timing, user roles, notices, and record responsibilities for the company’s access method.

  5. 05

    Escalate a personal, disputed, or rights question.

    Use the official mismatch and anti-discrimination resources, and seek qualified legal advice when the facts require individualized analysis.
