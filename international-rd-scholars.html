One program / USIVA companion

International R&D Scholars, with a clear visa-readiness scope.

This page is for people considering the US Fellows International R&D Scholars Program. It covers the planning questions around F-1 study, university coordination, authorized practical training, and responsible handoffs. It is not the program page and it is not legal advice.

  • Open program context↗
  • Return to all visa journeys

Scope before detail

Keep the research pathway and the visa question in their proper lanes.

US Fellows provides the research-program context. USIVA provides a public planning companion. The university, DSO, government agency, employer, sponsor, consulate, or qualified counsel remains responsible for the live decision.

USIVA covers

  • Organize records, dates, and questions across application, study, and post-completion stages.
  • Separate university-controlled assistantship, CPT, OPT, and STEM OPT questions before activity begins.
  • Point to primary government resources and the office or professional that must answer the live question.

This page does not

  • This page is not the program application, an admissions route, or a funding or employment promise.
  • It does not decide eligibility, provide individualized legal advice, file applications, or collect personal documents.
  • School, DSO, sponsor, employer, consular, agency, and qualified-counsel instructions control when they differ.

Four moments to verify

The question changes as the scholar moves.

Use the sequence as a conversation map. Do not treat it as a personal eligibility checklist or a substitute for current school and government instructions.

  1. 01 / Before applying

    Build the record

    Check identity, academic history, English-proficiency requirements, funding assumptions, prior visa history, and the school or program questions that still need an answer.

  2. 02 / Admission and visa

    Coordinate the F-1 sequence

    After a school decision, confirm the Form I-20 and SEVIS path, DS-160 information, financial evidence, interview preparation, and school-specific instructions.

  3. 03 / During study

    Keep activity authorized

    Treat the full course of study, assistantship, on-campus work, CPT questions, travel, and research-partner activity as separate items for DSO and university review.

  4. 04 / After completion

    Verify the next work question

    For OPT or possible STEM OPT, connect the degree, field, dates, employer, training plan, reporting duties, and current USCIS or DHS instructions before relying on a plan.

A hard boundary

Research exposure is not work authorization.

The program may create research or partner conversations. That exposure does not itself authorize employment, compensation, or training. Review the activity with the school, DSO, sponsor, employer, and appropriate counsel before it begins.

01

Assistantship

A university-controlled role with duties, funding, and status questions defined by the institution.

02

CPT

Curricular practical training that requires the applicable school authorization before activity begins.

03

OPT

Post-completion or other practical training questions tied to field, timing, filing, and authorization.

04

STEM OPT

An extension discussion that adds degree, employer, training-plan, and reporting requirements.
